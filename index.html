Sample Writing

Last winter, our school staged a play about a king who loses his crown, and I was cast as Guard Number Two. I had exactly one line: “Halt! Who goes there?” I practised it for weeks, in the shower, on the bus, even in my sleep, according to my sister.

On the night, the lights went down and the hall fell silent. When my moment came, I stepped forward, opened my mouth, and said, “Who goes halt?” A few people laughed. The king, my best friend, didn’t even blink. He just carried on, and somehow the scene survived.

What I learned that night is that a play has no retakes. Whatever happens on stage, happens in front of everyone. Strangely, that was the best part. Every second felt alive, and I have never felt more nervous or more proud.
